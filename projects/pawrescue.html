Objective

What the project set out to do

Bridge the gap between stray animals, rescuers, and adopters with one platform — replacing scattered social media posts with structured listings, verified shelter data, and a real adoption pipeline.

My Contribution

What I actually built

I directed the end-to-end development lifecycle using MongoDB, Express.js, and Node.js. I built the architecture for secure user authentication, multi-criteria forms (pet submission, adopter profiling), and resource schemas linking users to shelters and veterinary networks. I managed the project through five execution stages with formal change control, and deployed the live app to Render. As project lead I also owned scope, timeline, and stakeholder presentation.
