Objective

What the project set out to do

Give kayak rental businesses and customers a shared platform instead of manual bookings over phone/social media — with separate, purpose-built interfaces for each side of the transaction.

My Contribution

What I actually built

I built the full-stack booking platform using Node.js/Express.js on the backend and React on the frontend, with MongoDB for data storage. I implemented JWT-based authentication to separate customer and business-owner access, and built the interfaces each side needs — booking and browsing for customers, fleet and profile management for business owners.
